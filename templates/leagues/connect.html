How to find your SWID

Sign in to ESPN Fantasy in a desktop browser. Open Developer Tools (F12), select Application (Chrome/Edge) or Storage (Firefox), then Cookies for https://fantasy.espn.com or https://www.espn.com. Copy the SWID cookie value, including its braces.

How to find your espn_s2

In the same browser's Cookies section, find espn_s2 under an ESPN domain and copy its complete value. Both cookies must belong to the same signed-in ESPN account with access to your private league.

Leave both blank for public leagues. Credentials are used to access your private ESPN league. Treat these cookies like passwords. Never share them in screenshots, messages, or public repositories.
