Waiver Wire

How it works
  • Long-term pickup: With at least four forecast weeks available, the add/drop adds at least 2 projected lineup points per week on average and improves at least 60% of those weeks.
  • Short-term pickup: The add/drop improves this week or next week by at least 3 projected lineup points, but does not meet the sustained long-term threshold.
  • Short-term role: This player is benefiting from an injured teammate's absence; that workload may shrink when the teammate returns.
  • No lineup upgrade: The available projections do not show an improvement over an eligible bench drop.
  • Rising value: Recent snaps, targets/carries, or catches are trending up. Fading value means those usage signals are trending down.

Current-week points include injuries: Out, Doubtful and IR mean 0 points. Questionable means a 20% reduction.

Pickup tags are based on your connected roster and league lineup settings. Role trend filters describe recent usage and are separate from pickup fit. Missing predictions never count as zero when choosing a drop.

A dash means a prediction is unavailable. Season totals are hidden when weeks are missing, an injured player's return is unknown, or a pickup depends on a temporary role. QB, RB, WR and TE points use PPR scoring.
