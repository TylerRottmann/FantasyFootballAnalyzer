How it works
  • Long-term: A useful role for the rest of this season.
  • Short-term: An opportunity that may shrink when an injured regular returns.
  • Fading value: A shrinking role lowers the appeal of holding this player.
  • Uneven usage: Playing time varies from week to week.
  • Unclear: We need more information about how long the opportunity will last.

Current-week points include injuries: Out, Doubtful and IR mean 0 points. Questionable means a 20% reduction.

Long-term moves weigh season projections and roster depth. Short-term moves must improve this week’s lineup. Missing predictions never count as zero when choosing a drop.

A dash means a prediction is unavailable. Season totals are hidden when weeks are missing, an injured player's return is unknown, or a pickup depends on a temporary role. QB, RB, WR and TE points use PPR scoring.
